Privacy

ChaChing Support

Need help with your family, chores, allowance, or sign-in?

Email jesse@enormousbrain.com. Include your app version, iOS version, and what happened. Never send passwords, verification codes, or children's evidence photos.

Chores and photos

A child can report a chore done without a photo and add a note. Parents review those reports in the pending approvals list. Photo uploads require a parent's authorization and the uploader's permission in Privacy & Account.

Account deletion

Open Family > Privacy & Account as a parent, or the account button on Today as a child. Select Delete Account, read the explanation, and type DELETE to confirm. Contact support if you cannot access your account; we will verify your identity before changing or deleting data.

Notifications and widgets

Check notification permission in iOS Settings and reminder settings inside ChaChing. Widget updates depend on iOS background scheduling and may be delayed. Open the app to refresh immediately.

Payments

ChaChing tracks allowance and helps prepare payment requests. It does not automatically transfer money or confirm that an Apple Cash payment was received. A parent marks the allowance paid after making the payment.
