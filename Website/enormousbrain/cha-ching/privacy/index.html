ChaChing Privacy

Updated September 24, 2026

ChaChing helps families manage chores and allowance. This page describes how the app handles family information and how to request help or deletion.

Information used by the app

Supabase provides account authentication, database services, and private photo storage. Account information can include a phone number, email address, and Apple sign-in identifier. Family records include names, invitations, chore schedules, reviews, allowance history, savings goals, and notification preferences. Apple push notification tokens are stored when notifications are enabled.

Photo sharing and AI review

A parent must authorize cloud photo sharing, and the person uploading must agree before the upload. Photos are stored privately in Supabase and sent to OpenAI for AI-assisted chore review. Family parents can view and review evidence. AI results are advisory; parents make the final decision. You can report a chore without a photo for parent review.

When people blocking is enabled, person and face detection runs on the device before upload. Detection is imperfect. Do not include people, addresses, school details, or other private information in evidence photos.

Parents can stop family photo sharing under Family > Privacy & Account. Children can stop their own photo sharing from the account button on Today. This stops new uploads; existing photos still follow the family's retention settings.

Retention

Reviewed photos follow the family and chore deletion settings, including any grace period. A scheduled cleanup job removes due photos. Abandoned uploads become eligible for cleanup after 24 hours. Chore status, review decisions, and allowance records can remain after a photo is deleted. Provider backups and processing logs follow the providers' retention policies; deleting a photo in ChaChing is not a promise of immediate deletion from every provider backup or log.

Read more about Supabase privacy and OpenAI API data controls.

Location and notifications

Location and notification permissions are optional. Home reminder locations are saved on the device. Chore destinations and parent alert settings are stored as family data. Location reminders do not provide continuous location tracking. You can remove your saved home location and disable permissions in the app or iOS Settings.

Family access

Parents manage the children in their family. Family data is separated from other families. Do not share invitation links outside their intended recipients. Children should use the app with their parent's involvement.

Account deletion

Use Privacy & Account > Delete Account in the app. A child account deletion removes that child's profile, chores, photos, savings goals, and allowance history. When a parent deletes their account and another parent remains, the family continues and shared records remain with account attribution removed. Deleting the last parent removes the family and its child data. It does not delete other people's sign-in accounts.

Access is removed when deletion is accepted. Photo deletion and account removal retry automatically if a provider is temporarily unavailable. If Apple sign-in permission could not be revoked automatically, remove ChaChing in iPhone Settings > your name > Sign in with Apple.
